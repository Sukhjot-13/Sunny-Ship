# Interior reference contract — 2026-10-01

Sukhjot's supplied B1/F1/F2/F3 manga plans determine room placement. The readable
[Oda blueprint sheet](https://4.bp.blogspot.com/-XDb3Ozpx6YA/U-RHnsS3qxI/AAAAAAAACB8/DJpVz5pOdRY/s1600/artikel-Thousand_Sunny_all.jpg)
supplements their small resolution. The
[Thousand Sunny wiki](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Thousand_Sunny)
provides post-timeskip updates and room details.

- B1: forward storage, central radial Soldier Dock, paired aft workshops, stern Energy Room.
- F1: forward men's cabin; aft curved aquarium lounge and rear service passage.
- F2: women's cabin above the men's; aft dining and kitchen beside each other,
  with infirmary and pantry behind them.
- F3: observation deck and gardens above dining/kitchen; circular stern library
  with an attic washroom/toilet and separate bath above.
- Recognizable fittings: suspended double bunks, lockers, sunken kotatsu,
  entrance washrooms, king bed, wardrobe/vanity, mast cupboard/lift, restaurant
  bar, medical bed/swivel chair, encircling library bench and mapping desk.
- Post-timeskip dock channels 1–6: Shiro Mokuba I, Mini Merry II, Shark Submerge III,
  Kurosai FR-U IV, Brachio Tank V, inflatable pool. Old paddle compartments are removed.

The enlarged hull is an intentional experience setting. Human furniture, controls,
doors, and stairs use independent metre dimensions. Manga drawings do not supply
precise measurements for these rooms. Walking stairs replace some canonical ladder
connections to meet the requested ordinary-walking access. Those access adaptations
must remain explicit, rather than being described as exact manga geometry.

Detailed vehicle silhouettes, character poster artwork, book/map graphics, and
surface textures require a later visual pass. Bay labels establish the correct
post-timeskip assignments; they do not constitute finished vehicle models.
